Engraving an anniversary bracelet, a wedding band, a watch caseback or a photo pendant takes care: items are small, often already purchased and irreplaceable sentimentally. Because a laser marks without touching the piece, there is no clamping force or mechanical burin to scratch polished surfaces.

## Jewellery and personal items we engrave

- **Bracelets and bangles:** names, dates, coordinates, short messages or symbols on gold, sterling silver, stainless steel or leather.
- **Rings and wedding bands:** crisp lettering on either the outer band or the inner curve, legible even on slim rings.
- **Medals and photo pendants:** high-resolution portrait engraving of loved ones, pets, fingerprints or children's drawings on flat surfaces.
- **Watches and keepsakes:** watch backs, commemorative plaques, hip flasks, pocket knives and pet ID tags.

## Making small text, handwriting and photos look their best

On small items, legibility comes first:

- **Keep text concise on narrow pieces:** on a slim bracelet or inside a ring, a name and date or short line reads far more cleanly than a cramped paragraph. We advise on the best character height for the real space in millimetres.
- **Font selection:** overly ornate script fonts lose clarity at 2–3 mm tall; tell us the feel you want and we'll suggest clean, legible options.
- **Portraits on medals:** **bright, sharp close-ups** with clear contrast between the subject and background engrave much better than dark, distant or compressed photos. Always send the original image rather than a small screenshot.
- **Handwriting and drawings:** write or draw with a solid black pen on plain white paper, take a straight, well-lit photo and we'll reproduce your exact stroke on the metal.
