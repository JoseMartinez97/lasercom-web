---

Stainless steel is the metal most often engraved with a laser: plates, cutlery, flasks, tools, tags, cards and steel jewellery. Done well, the engraving does not wear off with use or cleaning, and the steel still does not rust. Here is what finishes there are, which one suits each piece and what we need to quote.

## Two finishes: a black mark or an engraving you can feel

We work all steel with the fibre laser and adjust the technique to what you need:

- **Annealing (black mark).** The laser heats the surface without removing material and darkens it. The result is a black or very dark grey mark, smooth to the touch and flush with the piece. The layer that protects the steel from corrosion stays intact, so it suits pieces that live outdoors or are washed often.
- **Engraving (removing material).** The laser removes a layer of steel and leaves a line with some depth that you can feel with your finger. It is lighter in tone than annealing and stands up better to later polishing.

If you are not sure which to choose, tell us what the piece is for and we will tell you.

## Stainless steel pieces we engrave

- **Plates:** rating, inventory, door, commemorative or identification plates for machinery.
- **Serial numbers, QR codes and barcodes:** with the contrast needed to read them with a scanner or a phone.
- **Flasks, bottles and cutlery:** names and logos for corporate gifts or events.
- **Tools and jigs:** marking who owns each piece or its reference so it does not go missing.
- **Tags and steel jewellery:** pet tags, bracelets, pendants and metal business cards.

## How to look after an engraved piece

Normal cleaning, water and detergents do not affect the mark. The only things that can lighten an annealed mark are aggressive polishing or a chemical surface treatment. If the piece will be polished afterwards, let us know: then it is better to engrave by removing material.
