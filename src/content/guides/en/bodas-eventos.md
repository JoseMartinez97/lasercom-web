At a wedding, christening, communion or falla celebration, the difference between generic favours and keepsakes guests actually keep comes down to real materials and genuine personalisation. With a laser, you don't pay for fixed printing plates: every item can carry the couple's names and date — or each individual guest's name — cleanly and permanently.

## Popular laser-cut and engraved event pieces

- **Place names and table markers:** cut-out names in wood or acrylic that guide guests to their seat and double as a keepsake to take home.
- **Engraved coasters, keyrings and fans:** useful pieces in birch plywood, cork or natural leather with your monogram, names and date in a toasted finish that never peels.
- **Frosted champagne and wine flutes:** toasting glasses engraved with the couple's names and wedding date in permanent frosted white that withstands washing.
- **Cake toppers and event signage:** laser-cut lettering in wood or mirror/coloured acrylic for cakes, welcome boards and seating corners.

## How we handle individual guest names

If you want each place card, coaster or glass personalised with a different guest's name:

1. **Send a clean guest list** (Excel, CSV or plain text, one name per line) checking accents, hyphens and double names carefully.
2. **Pick a font style** — or share your wedding invitation so we match the typography and look.
3. **Approve the digital proof** we send you for spelling, sizing and proportion before we cut or engrave the full run.

## Bringing your own glasses or fans

If you have already sourced your crystal flutes, bottles or wooden fans, you can bring them to the workshop for engraving. Send a photo and approximate dimensions first so we can verify machine clearance and material compatibility (for fans or boxes, confirming whether they are real wood or plastic).

## When to ask for a quote

As soon as you know your approximate guest count and event date. We have no minimum order — from two engraved flutes for the couple to favours for a full reception — and we fit in last-minute urgent additions whenever workshop capacity permits.
