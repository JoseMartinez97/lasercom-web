In industrial environments, adhesive stickers peel off with oil or heat and printed inks fade under solvents. Laser marking solves permanent identification on metal parts, tools and rating plates directly in the material — zero consumables and identical placement across every batch.

## What can be marked across a production batch

- **Sequential serial numbers:** each part receives a consecutive number or unique ID generated from your spreadsheet or numbering rule.
- **QR codes, DataMatrix and barcodes:** we calibrate module size, contrast and quiet zones so industrial and smartphone scanners read them reliably.
- **Rating and inventory plates:** part references, technical specs, owner details and logos marked onto pre-cut plates ready to rivet or bolt.
- **Logos and part references on components & tooling:** durable branding and inspection marks directly on tools, jigs or sub-assemblies.

## How different metals respond

We pair the laser source to the alloy and working environment:

- **Stainless steel (fibre laser annealing):** local thermal annealing produces a dark, high-contrast mark flush with the surface without removing metal or compromising corrosion resistance.
- **Anodised aluminium (CO2 or fibre laser):** removes the coloured anodised top coat to reveal bright bare aluminium — ideal for crisp barcodes, QR codes and small text.
- **Brass, copper, titanium and coated metals:** sharp, permanent direct marking without clamping marks or bending thin sheets.

## Custom parts cut to CAD plan

Alongside metal marking, Lasercom cuts custom parts for production lines — gaskets, spacers, flat housings, acrylic panels, wood or leather components — directly from your 1:1 millimetre CAD files (`DXF`, `DWG`, `SVG` or vector `PDF`).

For recurring orders, we retain your cutting files, fixtures and numbering rules so every repeat batch matches the first.
