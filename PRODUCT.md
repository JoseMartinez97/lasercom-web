# Product

## Platform
web

## Users
- **Individuals in Valencia and its metropolitan area** who want something personalised: wedding favours, an engraved bracelet for an anniversary (often urgent), a pet tag, a gift. Usually on a phone, coming from Google ("grabado láser Valencia", "grabar pulsera Valencia").
- **Businesses and industry**: serial numbers, barcodes and QR codes on parts, corporate gifts and merchandising with their logo, corporate letters and signs, parts cut to size.

## Product purpose
Lasercom is a laser cutting, engraving and marking workshop run by Vicente José Codosero García since 2007, at Polígono Industrial El Oliveral, calle L, nave 41, 46394 Riba-roja de Túria (Valencia). The site exists to rank on Google for laser cutting and engraving in Valencia and to turn visits into quote requests.

Success: a visitor sends a WhatsApp, calls or emails asking for a quote.

## Operating context

- Opening hours (Google Business Profile): Monday to Thursday 9:00–13:30 and 16:00–18:30, Friday 9:00–13:30.
- Prices are never published; every quote is personalised.

## Evidence on hand (true, usable)
- Founded 2007. Works with wood (solid, plywood, MDF), acrylic, glass (engraving), leather (CO2 laser), metals (stainless steel, anodised aluminium, brass, copper, titanium, silver, gold; CO2 and fibre).
- Work for Ron Barceló (stand of the 30th anniversary bottle, ~1.5 mm deep engraving) and Louis Vuitton (an exclusive fan for a charity auction and a run of fans), as stated on the old site.
- Google: 4.5 out of 5 from 6 reviews (November 2026). Three reviews quoted with names as published on Google.
- Photos in `src/assets/work/` are the workshop's own, from the old site.

## Absent, do not fabricate
Prices, turnaround times, machine models or bed sizes, delivery or shipping across Spain, client logos beyond the photos, extra reviews, certifications.

## Product principles
1. **Local first.** Valencia and nearby towns, address and phone visible on every page.
2. **Proof over promises.** Real pieces and real reviews.
3. **One clear next step.** Every page ends in WhatsApp, phone or email.
4. **Two audiences, one site.** Businesses and individuals each find their service quickly.
